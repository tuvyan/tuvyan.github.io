Help document words, pronunciation, orthography, and oral traditions

How contributions are reviewed

Community members may suggest a word, spelling, pronunciation, grammatical pattern, or oral narrative. Suggestions are not automatically added to the public dictionary. TUVYAN reviews supporting evidence and decides whether to approve publication.

Privacy: This form does not transmit or save your responses. It prepares text you can copy into GitHub Discussions. GitHub sign-in is required there. Please do not include private contact details, sensitive family information, or unpublished cultural knowledge without permission.

Prepare your contribution

Open TUVYAN Discussions

After copying, open Discussions, sign in to GitHub, start a discussion in the appropriate category, and paste the prepared text. If you do not have a GitHub account, read the community consultation instructions.

Community participation principles

Respect contributors and differences in regional speech; distinguish attested usage from personal preference; provide context for proposed spellings; avoid publishing private or culturally restricted information; and allow the editor to request clarification before approval.
