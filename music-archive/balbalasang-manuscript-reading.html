Balbalasang (Old Welsh Air)

Attributed on the source: Augustus U. Saboy. Tempo: Moderately. Source date: the inscription appears to end in “/99”; its full reading remains unverified.

Editorial status: This is a high-contrast reading facsimile of the historical score, not a verified note-for-note transcription. Unclear notes, chords and words have not been reconstructed or silently corrected.

Selected legible words
