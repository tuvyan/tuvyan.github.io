Banao Conservation History

From the 1974 reservation of land in Balbalasang for national park purposes to the Banao Protected Landscape Act of 2022: a documentary timeline, alongside family-history testimony concerning Gus Saboy's conservation legacy.

Timeline of the documentary record

1974 · December

National park reservation in Balbalasang

Presidential Proclamation No. 1357 reserved approximately 1,338 hectares of public-domain land in the then Barrio of Balbalasang, Municipality of Balbalan, Province of Kalinga-Apayao, for national park purposes, subject to existing private rights and more accurate survey.

Read the proclamation transcription

Date note: the supplied proclamation's heading identifies December 19, 1974, while its concluding execution clause states December 9, 1974. Both are retained in the source page pending official verification.

2022 · April 8

Banao Protected Landscape Act

Republic Act No. 11688 established the Banao Protected Landscape as a protected area under the National Integrated Protected Areas System (NIPAS), providing for its management and appropriating funds. Its provisions address governance, protected-area management, and conservation.

Gus Saboy and the conservation legacy

According to family testimony provided to TUVYAN, Gus Saboy's earlier efforts helped lay the groundwork for the later conservation legislation. The family also identifies former Congressman Allen Jesse Capuyan Mangaoang, described as a relative of Gus Saboy, as a proponent of the 2022 legislation.

These claims are recorded here as attributed historical testimony. To establish a fuller public history, the archive welcomes contemporaneous correspondence, legislative records, photographs, oral-history interviews, and other independently identifiable sources concerning the people and institutions involved.
