About TUVYAN

Writing, language, memory, and the places that shape us.

Welcome

TUVYAN is a space for essays, cultural memory, language documentation, and research. It brings together reflections on life and identity with the stories, voices, and histories of the Cordillera.

This page is an introduction to the work behind the website. A fuller personal account will be added here by the author.

Writing and research

The archive brings together personal writing, academic work, and historical materials. Its language documentation initiatives include ongoing work on Vinanaw (Vanaw) orthography and the bilingual dictionary, with community consultation forming part of the research process.

Heritage and community

Preserving family and community histories is one of TUVYAN's continuing purposes. Readers are invited to explore the Gus Saboy Memorial Archive and the Vanaw community consultation.
