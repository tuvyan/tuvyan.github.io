Vinanaw Orthography & Dictionary: Community Consultation

Welcome to the TUVYAN consultation space. Community members are invited to share feedback on draft spellings, word meanings, usage, pronunciation, and grammatical descriptions.

How to contribute

Describe the word or spelling you are commenting on, provide an example of its use where possible, and mention the relevant language variety or community if appropriate. Please avoid sharing personal or culturally restricted information without permission.

Draft status: Suggestions are contributions for review, not automatically approved dictionary entries or orthographic rules. Please do not treat comments as a final published standard.

New to online comments? Here is how to join

Joining is free. You only need an email address. You do not need to know how to use computer programming.

  1. Make a free account: Visit GitHub sign-up, enter your email, and follow the on-screen instructions to create an account. Check your email if GitHub asks you to confirm it.
  2. Come back to this page: Scroll down to Reader comments & discussion and click the sign-in button.
  3. Allow giscus: GitHub may ask whether you want to authorise giscus to post comments for you. Review the request and choose Authorize if you agree.
  4. Write your comment: Type your question, word suggestion, spelling correction, or example, then click the button to submit it.

Already have a GitHub account? Skip step 1 and just sign in below.

Privacy reminder: Comments are visible to everyone. Do not include phone numbers, private addresses, or culturally restricted knowledge.

If the sign-in window does not appear, try allowing pop-ups for this site or opening the page in another browser.

For longer proposals, you can also open a separate discussion on GitHub.
