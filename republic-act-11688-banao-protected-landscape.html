Republic Act No. 11688

Banao Protected Landscape Act

The Banao Protected Landscape Act declares a parcel of land in Balbalan, Kalinga, a protected area under the National Integrated Protected Areas System (NIPAS), with provisions for its management and funding. Section 1 identifies the short title of the Act.

This archive places the 2022 law alongside Presidential Proclamation No. 1357 (1974), which reserved land in Balbalasang for national park purposes. They represent two distinct legal instruments at different points in the area's conservation history; their precise legal relationship should not be assumed from the dates alone.

Community and family history

According to the account supplied to TUVYAN, the later legislation built on groundwork associated with Gus Saboy. The same account identifies former Congressman Allen Jesse Capuyan Mangaoang, a relative of Gus Saboy, as the bill's proponent. These attributions are presented as family/community historical context and have not been independently established from the pages of the law supplied here.
