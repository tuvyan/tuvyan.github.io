About this edition. The original chord sheets, including annotations and footnotes such as those for București, are reproduced as page images to preserve line breaks, chords, spelling, and annotations. Where the manuscript provides an English translation, it appears beside the original on wider screens and below it on phones.

Editorial note: the manuscript contents page lists 13 songs, but the full manuscript also contains “Ni Manong Ko.” “Dangdang-ay” and “Sorwe-e” share one sheet; both are indexed below for convenient access. The titles and source pages are preserved without assuming a revised album track order.

Nan-Asiwalak

Original lyrics & guitar chords

English translation · from the original manuscript

Diasporic/The Scattered Ones Here at the edges of the world, we the scattered ones live. There are plenty of good things to find here, but there's pain in our souls and bodies. It's heartbreaking to leave the place we grew up in: We always miss those we left behind. We get swamped by memories -- the hearth, the mountains, and the river. The hearth where we warm ourselves, the mountains where we hunt, the river where we bathe in. Here, we are but strangers. At times, all we get are insults/injustice. We just have to put our fists in our pockets, and get on with our work. It's heartbreaking to leave the place of our birth: We'll never know whether we're going back again. We just keep on remembering the hearth, the mountains and the river. The hearth where we warm ourselves, the mountains where we hunt, the river where we bathe in. In the village our kinsfolk are many. They gather to warm themselves around the fire. It’s a joy to chat with them; We don’t feel alone or cold. It’s hearbreaking to leave the community we got used to living in. When I shall join those who have gone ahead, I will just have to remember the hearth, the mountains and the river. The hearth where we warm ourselves, the mountains where we hunt, the river where we bathe in.
