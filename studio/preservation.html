Digital Preservation Audit

A read-only inspection of the repository's archived photographs, articles, media, and document links. This page cannot edit or delete your files.

Run an audit on your computer

  1. Download your full GitHub repository ZIP and extract it.
  2. Open Terminal in the extracted repository folder.
  3. Run python3 scripts/preservation_audit.py --root . --out preservation-report
  4. Open preservation-report/preservation-audit.json below. The file is read locally in your browser; it is not uploaded.

Audit summary

Review results carefully. Duplicate media may be intentionally reused. Missing links may be false positives for dynamic routes or case-sensitive paths. Undated records are not evidence of a new publication date.

Preservation priorities

  • Keep the weekly dated backup and an independent offline copy.
  • Review missing files and broken local references first.
  • Keep duplicate files until their use in articles has been checked.
  • Preserve original photo files, scans, dates, and provenance; do not replace them with generated images.
  • Check external videos, audio, and document links separately: they are not included in repository backups.
