Register existing papers and maintain publication dates without editing JSON manually.

This editor updates the public catalogue and its Studio copy. It does not upload a PDF or create an article page. First upload the paper through the Article Editor or GitHub, then register its existing HTML filename here. Use verified publication dates; an upload date is not a first-publication date.
