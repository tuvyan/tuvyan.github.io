AUGUSTUS U. SABOY PAPERS

by CRISTINA B. VILLANUEVA, Archivist

UNIVERSITY OF THE PHILIPPINES BAGUIO

Scope and Contents

Notes

The Augustus U. Saboy papers are digital copies of documents that were initially deposited in the UP Baguio Cordillera/Northern Luzon Historical Archives in 2010 by his son, Scott, a former faculty member of the College of Arts and Communication of UP Baguio.

Saboy’s papers consist mostly of materials on the bodong and pagta, the Kalinga indigenous peace and conflict settlement process, as well as sources relative to the Cordillera autonomy movement.

Over 9,000 of the collection are photographs and film negatives. The photos depict Philippine political events documenting campaign efforts for Cordillera autonomy, Kalinga community affairs, Baguio City growth and development activities and Saboy family milestones.

Biographical Note

Augustus Saboy, or Gus as he was fondly called, was a Tinguian born on May 3, 1928, in Balbalasang, Balbalan, Kalinga. He received his BS Education degree from Silliman University in 1953 and his Bachelor of Arts degree in 1963 from the Baguio College Foundation, where he also attended 2 years of law school.

Gus Saboy was a journalist by profession, having worked in various capacities as news reporter, editor, correspondent, and writer in both government print and broadcast media as well as private organizations.

Among the media outfits he worked for are The Baguio Midland Courier, Philippine News Service, Philippine News Agency, Baguio Daily Vibrations, The Highland Sentinel, and Radyo ng Bayan.He served as executive assistant in the Office of the Governor.

He held the positions of provincial secretary, provincial administrator, and provincial planning and development coordinator in Kalinga Apayao. He was appointed as Bureau Chief of the Philippine News Agency in 1984 and station manager of the Radyo ng Bayan Kalinga Apayao in 1986.

In 1989, he was designated provincial executive officer of the Cordillera Bodong Administration. He also served as Executive Director of the Cordillera Executive Board.A well-known scholar and student of Cordillera history, he has written numerous articles on Cordillera history and culture.

He was an active participant in the peace process of the Cordillera. His integrity, long stint, broad and multifaceted experience in both public and private service have earned him the respect and trust of people and leaders of the Cordillera.He was married to Anatalia Magkachi Saboy, a retired DECS Education Supervisor, with whom he had 4 children.
