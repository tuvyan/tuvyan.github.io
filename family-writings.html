Editorial principles

Original manuscripts and published pages are retained as primary evidence. A transcription is identified as a transcription, and an enhanced image is not presented as an untouched original. Works written by a family member are distinguished from works written about them; collaborators and original publication dates are acknowledged when supported by the source.

Additional non-musical writings can be added as individual pages after their text, authorship and dates are checked against the archival documents.
