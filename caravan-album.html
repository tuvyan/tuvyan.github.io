Original announcement

Finally, after 3 years of off and on work on this personal project, our music album “CARAVAN” is now live on Spotify and other platforms!

Massive thanks to Josiah Anivyan Saboy for his patience and incredible studio work! Also thanks to Ann Fuentes Saboy for her unending support, and Vashti Nicole F. Savuy for performing my first two songs.

Shout out too to my childhood friend, Edison L. Balansi (Sodein LB) for inspiring me to write my own songs and for producing ‘Nan-asiwalak’ and ‘Gift of a Lifetime’.

I got a long list of relatives and friends who have encouraged me in this project, and so even if I can't mention you by name, let me thank you wherever you are.

NB: I'm not a professional singer, so whatever imperfections there are in the recordings are of my own doing.
