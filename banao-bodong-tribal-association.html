THE BANAO BODONG ASSOCIATION (BBA) SMALL-SCALE GOLD MINING IN GA-ANG MINES TALALANG, BALBALAN KALINGA CAR PHILIPPINES

by ROYCE LINGBAWAN

Former BBA Chairman

Presented at the

Asia-Pacific Regional Conference on Artisanal

and Small-Scale Mining

in Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia

The Banao Bodong Association (BBA) is the precursor to the Banao Bodong Tribal Association (BBTA),the Vanaw community's governing body covering matters related to the VOJÓNG (BODÓNG) 'peace pact' -- i.e., relations with other ethnolinguistic group in and outside and the GAANG community mines.
